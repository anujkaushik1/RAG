# Order Status Guide

Every order in your account has a status. This is what each one means.

## pending
The order has been placed and is waiting for payment or for the fund
house to confirm it. Most orders move out of pending within 1 working day.

## paid
Payment was received and the order was sent to the fund house. For a
purchase, units are usually allotted within 1 to 2 working days.

## failed
The order did not go through, and no units were bought. The most common
reasons are insufficient balance in the bank account, a bank mandate that
is not yet approved, or a payment timeout. Any money debited for a failed
order is refunded within 5 to 7 working days. Failed SIP instalments are
not retried automatically; the next instalment happens on its normal date.

## paused
The SIP is on a scheduled pause and no debits will happen until the pause
ends. See the SIP Pause Policy for details.

## Order types
- `purchase_sip`: a SIP instalment or SIP registration
- `pause_sip`: a request to pause a SIP
- `redeem`: a request to sell units
