# SIP Pause Policy

## Eligibility

Only an active SIP can be paused.

A SIP cannot be paused if a cancellation request is pending.

## Cutoff

A pause request must be submitted at least five business days
before the next debit date.

## Duration

A SIP can be paused for one to three instalments.

## After the pause ends

When the pause period ends, the SIP becomes active again on BSE.
